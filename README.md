# 🧱 BrickBurst Plus

**Play. Spot a pattern. Come back stronger.**

A browser arcade game with 30 progressively challenging Adventure levels, rotating daily missions, unlockable ball colours and visual gameplay insights.

## 🎮 Play Now

**[▶ Play BrickBurst Plus](https://gfolomo.github.io/BrickBurst-Plus/)**

Play with a mouse, touch controls or keyboard. No account or installation is required.

This is the expanded edition of **[BrickBurst](https://github.com/GFolomo/BrickBurst)**. The original project and its [playable version](https://gfolomo.github.io/BrickBurst/) remain available separately.

## 🌈 Adventure: 30 Levels, Three Worlds

| World | Levels | What Changes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🌈 **First Bounce** | 1–10 | Open layouts introduce paddle control and shot angles, with boss gates along the way. |
| 🔥 **Trick Shots** | 11–20 | Moving formations, tougher bricks and more precise shots. |
| 🌌 **Cosmic Chaos** | 21–30 | Key-and-shield mechanics combined with moving formations and boss gates. |

Challenge increases automatically through ball speed, paddle size and brick patterns. Occasional lighter stages provide breathing room. There is no difficulty selector, and the progression does not secretly change in response to individual wins or losses.

Every fifth Adventure level includes a moving boss core. Break green **KEY** bricks to open shielded bricks marked **◆**.

## ☀️ A Different Daily Mission

Daily Challenges change how you play, alongside the layout, colours and background. Eight mission types rotate; a type can return on a later date, while consecutive days have different missions.

| Mission | Your Goal | The Twist |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🎯 **Star Seeker** | Break six star-marked bricks. | A small paddle rewards precise angles; other bricks are optional. |
| ⏱️ **Ninety-second Dash** | Clear the court within 90 seconds of active play. | A faster ball, softer bricks and a clock shared across lives. |
| 🛡️ **Rally Survivor** | Survive 60 seconds and make 12 paddle returns. | Armoured bricks regrow when the court is cleared. |
| 🔑 **Crack the Vault** | Break both keys, then destroy the core. | The moving core stays shielded until both keys are gone. |
| 💥 **Chain Reaction** | Clear the explosive court within 100 seconds. | Neighbouring explosive bricks create chain reactions. |
| 👑 **Core Pursuit** | Hit the moving core nine times within 100 seconds. | Lead the target and ignore the decoys. |
| ☄️ **Double Trouble** | Clear the court. | Each launch starts two balls; a life is lost when both drop. |
| 🌬️ **Wind Tunnel** | Clear the staggered court. | Changing side wind bends the ball’s path. |

A briefing explains the goal and twist, with live progress during play.

- **One run per local calendar day:** winning, losing all lives, running out of time in a timed mission, or confirming “End today’s attempt” closes that day’s challenge.
- **Take a break:** Home saves an unfinished Daily run so it can be resumed with its remaining lives.
- **Daily refresh:** the invitation appears on the first eligible visit each day. A new challenge is available the following day.
- **Consistent daily rules:** a given date generates the same mission and layout, independently of Adventure progression. Dates follow the device’s local calendar.
- **Playtest preview:** “Preview tomorrow” lets testers explore the next mission without recording scores or consuming a daily attempt.

The daily lock and saved progress use browser storage and the device clock; they are not enforced by a server or synchronised across devices.

## ⭐ Stars and Your Ball Collection

Adventure awards **3 stars for a clear with no missed balls**, **2 for one missed ball**, or **1 for completing the level**. Misses include individual balls dropped during multi-ball play. Your best star rating for each level counts towards cosmetic unlocks.

| Ball Colour | Unlock |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🟡 Solar Gold | Available immediately |
| 🟢 Mint Comet | 5 stars |
| 🌸 Rose Rocket | 15 stars |
| 🟣 Cosmic Violet | 30 stars |

Choose an unlocked colour on Home. Cosmetics do not change speed or strength; Daily missions use their own themed ball colour.

## 🕹️ Controls and Progress

| Action | Control |
| :--- | :--- |
| Move the paddle | Mouse movement, touch-drag, or **← / →** |
| Launch the ball | Click, tap, or **Space** |
| Pause or resume | **Pause**, **P**, or **Escape** |

- **Adventure → Home:** ends the run. Starting Adventure again begins the selected level fresh, with a new score and three lives.
- **Closing and reopening:** Adventure starts at the saved level, rather than resuming mid-flight.
- **Pause or switching away:** preserves the current play session.
- **Daily → Home:** saves the unfinished run. Explicitly ending the attempt requires confirmation.
- Unlocked levels, star ratings, selected ball colour and best scores are stored locally in the browser.

Capsules: **W** widens the paddle, **M** adds balls, **S** slows the ball and **+** adds a life. **✸** bricks explode. Daily missions drop only **W** and **S**.

## 📊 Data Analytics Behind BrickBurst Plus

BrickBurst Plus is a browser game that turns gameplay data into visual feedback. It demonstrates how data analytics can help players understand their performance, recognize patterns, and decide what to try next.

### 🎮 What Does the Game Track?

- 🎯 **Points earned** — scoring performance across attempts and levels.
- 🧱 **Bricks destroyed** — progress through each level.
- 🔥 **Best combo** — consecutive bricks destroyed before a paddle return or lost life.
- ⚡ **Power-ups collected** — bonuses caught during play.
- 🏓 **Paddle returns and rallies** — how often players return the ball and sustain play.
- ⏱️ **Active playing time** — time spent playing, excluding launch screens and results.
- 📍 **Miss locations** — where balls slip past the paddle.
- 🌡️ **Ball activity** — where the ball spends time across the court.

### 💡 Why Collect This Data?

A final score tells players how they finished, but it offers limited guidance on how to improve.

BrickBurst Plus makes performance visible through automatic results after a lost life, a cleared level or the end of a game. Players can spot patterns, try a different approach, and review what happens next.

**🎮 Play → 📊 Analyse → 💡 Adjust → 🔁 Try again**

### 🔍 Insights and Actions

| Visual or Metric | Insight | Action a Player Can Try |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🌡️ **Ball hotspots** | Shows where the ball spends the most time. | Anticipate busy areas and experiment with paddle angles to reach quieter corners. |
| 📍 **Miss locations** | Reveals whether dropped balls cluster on the left, middle or right. | Position the paddle earlier and practise covering the most frequently missed area. |
| 🏓 **Paddle contacts** | Shows how often the ball hits the centre or edges of the paddle. | Practise controlled centre returns or use edge shots for wider angles. |
| 📊 **Points per level** | Shows how scoring is distributed across the run. | Identify levels worth practising and explore different approaches. |
| 📈 **Attempt score trail** | Shows how scores change between attempts. | Test a strategy and compare subsequent attempts within the same level. |
| 🔥 **Combos and rallies** | Highlights consecutive brick clears and sustained ball returns. | Aim for longer rallies while experimenting with controlled shots. |
| ⚡ **Power-ups collected** | Shows how many bonuses the player caught. | Practise catching useful power-ups while keeping the ball in play. |

### 🧠 Reading the Results

- **After a lost life or level clear:** stats and maps describe the latest attempt.
- **At game over or victory:** stats and maps describe the whole run.
- **Points per level and the attempt trail:** show progress across the current run.
- **Heat-map colours:** show time spent in each area, rather than collision counts. Brighter cells indicate more activity.

The visualisations describe **what happened**, rather than proving **why it happened**. Level difficulty, remaining bricks and power-ups can affect comparisons. A pattern offers a useful starting point for experimentation.

### 🛠️ Data Skills Demonstrated

- **Data collection:** capturing gameplay events and ball positions.
- **Data aggregation:** summarising performance by attempt, level and run.
- **Spatial analysis:** grouping ball activity and misses by court location.
- **Data visualisation:** presenting patterns through charts, heat maps and summary cards.
- **Descriptive analytics:** identifying scoring, movement and performance patterns.
- **Actionable communication:** translating findings into short, practical gameplay tips.

BrickBurst Plus applies data analytics to an interactive experience, helping players turn each attempt into an opportunity to learn and improve.

## 🎯 From Insight to Practice

The results screen offers a short practice court based on the side with the most missed balls. Players can practise covering that area, then try the skill in Adventure. Practice does not award Adventure stars.

## 💾 Run Locally

Download `index.html` and open it in a modern browser. The game is a self-contained HTML, CSS and JavaScript file; no build tools or local server are required.

Progress belongs to the browser and website origin. Opening a downloaded file, using a different browser, or playing on another device may use separate saves. Clearing browser storage removes saved progress.
