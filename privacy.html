BrickBurst Plus Privacy Policy

Eagliz Digital · Effective 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Android game BrickBurst Plus (package com.gfolomo.brickburstplus), provided by Eagliz Digital. It describes the current offline version, which has no advertising, in-app purchases, account registration or developer-operated online analytics service. It does not describe other apps.

Information used during play

The game processes scores, bricks destroyed, paddle returns, combos, power-ups, playing time, ball activity, misses and attempts on your device. These produce your results, heatmaps and improvement tips. It also uses your device's local date for daily challenges.

Best scores, unlocked levels, stars, selected ball colour, daily challenge results, invitation dates and an unfinished daily run are saved in the app's local storage. Session records and gameplay statistics are processed locally; daily checkpoints can include the information needed to resume that run. These records are not uploaded to Eagliz Digital.

Collection and sharing

The current game does not send gameplay records or personal information to us, sell user data, or share gameplay data with advertisers. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos to play. No ad SDK, payment SDK, remote analytics SDK or cloud-save service is integrated in this version. An internet permission is present in the Android wrapper, but the bundled game works offline and is configured to block gameplay network uploads.

Storage, retention and deletion

Saved progress remains in local app storage until it is overwritten by gameplay, cleared or removed. A daily checkpoint is removed when its run finishes. Other saved results may remain until you clear app data or uninstall. Some session-only records are discarded when the app process ends. There is no developer-hosted player account or server copy of your progress to delete or restore.

To delete all local game data, use your phone's Settings → Apps → BrickBurst Plus → Storage → Clear data/Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Menu labels vary by phone. This resets progress, scores and unlocks and cannot be undone by us. Normal app updates are intended to preserve local progress; clearing data or uninstalling does not.

If you contact us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use these to answer your enquiry, troubleshoot issues and manage support, rather than for advertising. Correspondence is handled by our email provider and retained as needed to resolve the enquiry, maintain relevant support records or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any records we must retain. Do not send passwords, identity documents or payment information.

Google Play and this policy website

Google Play and Android may separately process installation, purchase-account, diagnostic or test-program information under Google's policies and your settings. That is distinct from the game's local gameplay insights. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The public web copy of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website makes a normal web request to GitHub, which may process technical request information such as IP addresses under its own policies. We have not added advertising or analytics trackers to this policy page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Reading the copy bundled inside the Android app does not require visiting the website.

Security and younger players

Local storage relies on your device's app storage protections. Protect your device and install updates from trusted sources; no storage or software can be guaranteed completely secure. The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. The current version has no accounts, chat or advertising and does not ask players of any age to submit personal information to play.

Changes to the game or this policy

If we introduce ads, purchases, accounts, online features or other data handling, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures before releasing those features, and provide any notices or consent choices required. The effective date above identifies this policy version.
